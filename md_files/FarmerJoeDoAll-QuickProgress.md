# Farmer Joe Quick Progression

1. Complete beginner setup: Tutorial badges, Battle Oracle Hood, Battle Oracle Wings, Battle Oracle Battlestaff, level 10, Rogue, level 25, Ninja, and Mage.
2. Set the best available solo, farm, and dodge classes.
3. Complete Elders' Blood daily when available.
4. Optionally collect REP, XP, and 10-minute boosts.
5. Level to 30, then get and rank 10 Master Ranger and Dragonslayer.
6. Farm Blade of Awe REP, buy Awethur's Accoutrements, and unlock Forge Helm Enhancement.
7. Level to 50, then get and rank 10 Scarlet Sorceress and Dragonslayer General.
8. Get Burning Blade.
9. Level to 55, then get and rank 10 Blaze Binder and Cryomancer.
10. Level to 60, then get and rank 10 DragonSoul Shinobi and unlock Lacerate.
11. Level to 65, then get and rank 10 Glacial Berserker.
12. Level to 70, then get Horc Evader, unlock Smite, Vim, Pneuma, Examen, and Anima, and get and rank 10 ArchPaladin.
13. Level to 75, then get Archfiend DoomLord and Archfiend.
14. After Elders' Blood is complete, attempt to acquire Void Highlord at the level 80 bracket.
15. Prepare a healing class, acquire and equip Cape of Awe, and complete the 13 Lords of Chaos.
16. Get and rank 10 Lord of Order, Frost Spirit Reaver, Northlands Monk, and Shaman when available.
17. Unlock Forge Weapon Enhancement and Praxis.
18. Level to 80 and complete the Celestial Arena quest line.
19. Get Burning Blade of Abezeth.
20. Unlock Forge Cape Enhancement, Absolution, Vainglory, Avarice, and Lament.
21. Get Yami No Ronin and Dragon of Time.
22. Level to 100.
23. Get King's Echo.
24. Get Hollowborn Reaper's Scythe.
25. Prepare endgame enhancements: Hero's Valiance, Elysium when eligible, and Ravenous when eligible.
26. Complete Exalted Apotheosis prerequisites.
27. Max Nation materials through Supplies.
28. Set the best available classes and prepare the Farmer Joe outfit: NO BOTS Armor, Scarecrow Hat, The Server is Down, and Smart Enhancement.
29. Equip the full outfit when the outfit option is enabled.
30. Acquire and equip Hot Mama or Akriloth Pet when selected in the pet configuration.
